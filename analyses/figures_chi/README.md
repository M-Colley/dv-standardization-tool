# OpenDV-HCI — CHI figure set

Generated by [`opendv/analysis/make_chi_figures.py`](../../opendv/analysis/make_chi_figures.py) from the
39-study run of `sources_manifest_no4tu.yaml` (2026-11-15, after the LLM-mapping audit). Every number in the figure
captions is computed from the run artifacts.

**Pipeline that produced the inputs** (commands in `docs/reproducibility.md`)

1. `opendv batch --manifest sources_manifest_no4tu.yaml --snapshot-manifest --cache-dir data/processed/cache`
   - all 39 development studies: 11 fetched from their repositories, 28 pinned downloads in `data/raw/development/`;
   - local-LLM fallback after header matching, with the plausibility check (rules v1.3) and the `definition` shortlist;
   - model: `google/gemma-4-E2B-it`, the largest default model that fits the 16 GB GPU used.
2. `opendv analyze --input-dir <run>/standardized --output-dir analyses/output_python_full --exclude-llm-deduced`
3. `python -m opendv.analysis.multiverse_analysis --input-dir <run>/standardized` → `analyses/output_multiverse/`
4. `python -m opendv.analysis.make_chi_figures` → this folder (vector **PDF** + 300-dpi **PNG** per figure).
   Fig. 1 and Fig. 6 take their counts from the batch run that step 2 analysed (the `input_dir` in
   `analysis_results.json`); `OPENDV_BATCH_DIR` overrides it. Fig. 4 reads the `*_llm_excluded` tables
   of step 2, so the script stops if step 2 ran without `--exclude-llm-deduced`.

## Methods caveats (read before using the figures)

1. **LLM-deduced mappings were audited; none is pooled now.** In the
   leave-alias-out benchmark the LLM gives the curated DV for 36% of the DV
   columns of the first 23 studies (41% before the audit added curated
   columns) and 17% of the 16 added ones (`analyses/output_llm_evaluation/`).
   In situ it was worse: of the 378 LLM-mapped columns the all-mappings
   analysis pooled until 2026-11-14 (56 of its 88 DVs had LLM rows), 34 had
   the right DV, 9.0% [6.5, 12.3] (Wilson; `analyses/llm_mapping_audit/`).
   Since the audit's fixes (2026-11-15) the 17 LLM answers left are all set
   aside beside a curated column of their DV (`llm_columns_set_aside.csv`), so
   the all-mappings tables equal the `*_llm_excluded` ones: 62
   meta-analyzable DVs, 49 with k ≥ 3. Fig. 1, Fig. 2, Fig. 3 and the PCA
   composite use all mappings, which now means the same columns. Fig. 4 is
   drawn from the LLM-excluded pool since 2026-11-14. Its all-mappings pool
   had two LLM rows until they were found to be other questionnaires and
   locked (2026-11-14): FOV Snap-Turn's `Mechanics1_2`–`Mechanics1_5`
   (VRNQ game-mechanics items, 28.9% of the weight) and Gaze Platformer's
   `Enjoyment_fun` and `Enjoyment_entertaining` (PXI enjoyment items, 5.0%);
   the pool went from k = 21, 7.73 [6.41, 9.05] to k = 19, 8.14 [6.83, 9.45]
   (HKSJ), and to 8.13 [6.80, 9.45] when Running into Traffic's 0–21 NASA-TLX
   was put on 0–20 (2026-11-15). Until 2026-11-14 SenseCobot's EEG beta/alpha ratios, LLM-mapped to mental
   demand, were pooled together with its NASA-TLX ratings (study mean 0.39 of
   20, 17.5% of the weight); its row is now the rating (6.98, 0.3%).
2. **Observation-level n.** Meta-analysis weights use row counts, not
   participants, so large row-level exports (TLX formats with 3,888 rows,
   FACT-AV with 2,600) weigh heavily. The participant-level alternative is one axis of the multiverse
   (`analyses/output_multiverse/`). Since 2026-11-15 osf_cwd6h's perceived risk
   adds 1,811,262 samples of 27 participants; in its random-effects pool (τ² = 76)
   the three studies still weigh about the same, but the PCA composite, which
   weights rows, now has it as its first component (82.9% of the variance).
3. **Scale harmonization decides many estimates.** Six of the added studies
   record the NASA-TLX on 0–100. Pooled on their raw scales the subscales
   move from 6–8 to 13–23 on the schema's 0–20, and `perceived_safety` from 1.38
   to 2.72, depending on this one choice. `understanding_rating` (3.80–5.73
   until 2026-11-15) no longer depends on it: its spread came from FACT-AV's
   SART understanding sum (3–21), which the audit locked.
4. **Small k.** Many pooled DVs have k = 2–3. Use the HKSJ interval
   (`hksj_ci95_low/high`) rather than the Wald interval for inference.
5. **Measurement invariance.** No shared scale reaches even configural
   invariance: SUS, both Van der Laan scales, perceived safety and both
   UEQ-S scales misfit, and the NASA-TLX model cannot be fitted because one
   study's physical demand has no variance (column `measurement_invariance`
   in `meta_analysis_summary.csv`; `measurement_invariance.csv`). Treat
   pooled means, including Fig. 4's Mental Demand, as describing where
   studies sit rather than as comparable measurements; `docs/cross_study_insights.md`
   pools correlations instead.
6. **Changes relative to the 11-study figure set of 2026-10-25.** 28 studies
   added (2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05); plausibility rules v1.3 and header
   matching; perceived safety scored from its items; Van der Laan keying of
   ROADS and eHMI-for-All fixed; the scale detection no longer reads
   off-range scales as 0–20. See `docs/changelog.md`.
7. **Fig. 1 and Fig. 6 of 2026-11-08 showed the wrong run.** They were drawn
   from the 23-study batch run of 2026-11-04 (23 sources, 1,781 files, 282
   LLM-deduced aliases) next to 39-study analysis numbers, because the figure
   script read `data/processed/catalog_meta_analysis` unless told otherwise.
   It now reads the run the analysis read. Fig. 2 and Fig. 3 are sized by the
   number of studies since 2026-11-14; at 39 studies their labels overlapped,
   and Fig. 3 now shows its cell values only while they fit
   (`dv_overlap_matrix.csv` has them).
